Data and rendering methods

Each complete dataset is independently min–max normalized. Orange-red runs from light (low) to dark (high); gray marks missing or masked values. Phase plots retain cyclic colors. Zero is the sample's minimum, not zero fluorescence; colors do not compare absolute expression across samples.

Author matrices, video extraction, binned tracks and color reconstruction are different data sources. Grayscale movies retain display intensity; pseudocolor reconstruction only approximates a color-scale index and may exclude pixels that cannot be matched. Neither recovers raw fluorescence. Open each card's Figure & sampling for its ROI, registration, channel, calibration and any smoothing or derived variants.

Each plot retains its own coordinates, units and extracted time range. This may cover only a subset of the source movie; check the card's frame and time summary. Missing values remain missing.

Videos and source ranges

Choose Videos in a paper dialog. Only the selected movie loads when you press Play. Speed, seek and available ROI overlays are supported. Playback time is not experimental time. Range & source files lets you crop stored time points without rescaling colors or filling missing values.

Library and local notes

Redrawn means the paper has a comparison dataset. Need data means no redraw is available yet; Reference identifies background/method papers. Notes stay in this browser and origin. Export JSON before changing browser or website address.
